# X Shortcut Plus 1.1.3-beta.1

Allows modified account shortcuts, including `Ctrl+Alt+a`, in X’s photo/video viewer and `/compose/post` reply composer.

These routes activate dialog and editing guards. X also marks the background sidebar `aria-hidden`, which previously hid the active account and account-menu button from the extension. The revised account action can use those background controls. It permits the media/composer dialogs that existed when the action started and its own account panel. Other shortcuts retain their dialog and editing guards. Unmodified account bindings, active input-method composition, repeated keys, and unrelated dialogs remain protected.

The action restores the URL after switching. It does not copy unsent reply text into the other account.
