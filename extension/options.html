Feed shortcuts

Use X’s native . shortcut to load new posts. Top and new posts is optional and unassigned by default.

Ctrl+Alt+1–9 selects feeds by displayed position. Ctrl+Alt+u opens the selected post’s author. Ctrl+Alt+a switches to the next signed-in account and restores this tab’s URL.

Pinned lists

Open Home on X to discover pinned lists. Each account has its own list bindings. Reordering or renaming a list keeps its binding.

Next and previous follow the displayed feed order and wrap at either end. The account shortcut follows X’s signed-in account menu order. URL and Home feed restoration do not preserve the exact scroll position.

Your shortcut settings, X username, and discovered list names and IDs are stored locally in this browser. Account switching temporarily stores the return URL and target account in this X tab. Privacy policy.
